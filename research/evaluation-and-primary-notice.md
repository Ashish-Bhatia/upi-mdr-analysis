# Edition 07 — Original NPCI deferral and DFS–Ipsos incentive evaluation

**Evidence cutoff: 16 October 2026, India.** This audit corrects two retrieval gaps from earlier editions and tests government claims against their own documentation.

## 1. Official implementation status finally retrieved

The [NPCI original 13 October 2026 single-page PDF](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/UPI_MDR_on_Select_Merchant_Transactions_Shall_be_Implemented_Post_Diwali_4c9cdd77d5.pdf) was directly opened on NPCI's domain and visually inspected. The **UPI and Services Steering Committee**, citing the festive season, decided that the new MDR on select P2M payments would be implemented after Diwali at a date to be notified separately.

**Verified finding:** 15 October 2026 is **not** an implemented start date. **Unresolved:** post-Diwali effective date, any circular replacing or amending OC-237, precise reactivation requirements and merchant opt-out behavior. A newspaper's possible 1 January 2027 date is speculation. Paytm's signed October 13 disclosure is corroboration, not a substitute for the now available NPCI original.

The press release is not an authenticated complete copy of original NPCI UPI OC-237: those are different documents, and the four-page OC-237 remains a third-party-hosted original-form copy.

## 2. Government's original adoption evaluation

The [Department of Financial Services PIB announcement, 16 February 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2228651), identifies a report prepared by **Ipsos Research Pvt Ltd**, in consultation with NPCI, for the DFS digital incentive programme. Methodology stated by the Ministry:

| Cohort | Surveyed |
|---|---:|
| Users | 6,167 |
| Merchants | 2,199 |
| Payment service providers | 2,012 |
| **Total** | **10,378** |
| Coverage | 15 states; North, South, East, West and Northeast zones |
| Fieldwork | 22 July–25 August 2025 |
| Interview type | Face-to-face CAPI |

The announcement further describes **94% of surveyed small merchants adopting UPI**, **72% of merchants reporting satisfaction**, **57% of merchants reporting greater sales following digital adoption** and **57% of surveyed users preferring UPI** compared with 38% preferring cash. These are survey cohort observations under the previous zero-MDR environment, not national-census totals, predicted price elasticity or independent causal evidence that subsidies alone explain behaviour.

The Ministry's linked [full impact analysis PDF](https://financialservices.gov.in/beta/sites/default/files/socio-economic-impact-incentive-scheme.pdf) could be found in indexed excerpts but yielded 404/502 when directly requested. Hence this review **does not certify every page, detailed weights, questionnaire, model or annexure**.

### Two headline '76%' statistics cannot be merged

- The indexed DFS–Ipsos report says approximately **76% of its respondents would revert to cash IF UPI became unavailable entirely**. Fieldwork 2025.
- [LocalCircles' October 2026 consumer survey](https://www.localcircles.com/a/press/page/upi-mdr-consumer-survey) says **76% of 37,654 respondents expected to prefer other payment methods IF UPI carried an extra cost**.

Both are hypothetical self-reports, with *different treatments* and populations. They are neither observed consequences of the September MDR nor independent replication of the same outcome.

## 3. Reconciliation of RuPay and UPI incentives

The [March 19 2025 Cabinet original record](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2112771) lists category-level payouts for FY22–FY24. The [February 2026 DFS evaluation announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2228651) lists combined total including FY2024–25.

| Year | Combined disbursed | RuPay debit | BHIM-UPI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| FY2021–22 | 1,389 | 432 | 957 |
| FY2022–23 | 2,210 | 408 | 1,802 |
| FY2023–24 | 3,631 | 363 | 3,268 |
| FY2024–25 | 1,046 | not separately verified | not separately verified |
| **FY22–FY25** | **8,276** | **1,203 identified FY22–FY24** | **6,027 identified FY22–FY24** |

The difference between FY2024–25 Cabinet **approved estimated ₹1,500-crore** programme outlay and evaluation's **₹1,046-crore reported disbursement** is **₹454 crore arithmetically**, not an independently proved unused budget amount. Actual accounting explanations, fiscal release dates and settled merchant claims remain unverified.

## 4. Distinguish four levels of evidence

1. **Original institution statement:** primary evidence that NPCI officially postponed rollout and DFS reported study results.
2. **Third-party study commissioned by Government:** credible fieldwork provenance but not an uncontrolled claim of causation. Questionnaire, weighting and counterfactual evaluation must be reviewed.
3. **Media coverage and hypothetical surveys:** evidence of claims/intentions; not interchangeable with measured behavior after a policy starts.
4. **Revenue and pricing models:** conditional scenarios whose results depend on merchant ticket-bin and exception assumptions.

This audit tests whether the proposed MDR protects established adoption benefits, but *does not* assume either that the 2020–25 subsidy was entirely successful or that a new fee necessarily damages adoption. A rigorous next-stage model must compare actual eligible merchant fees, tax incidence, infrastructure funding, consumer/merchant response, and taxpayer cost.
