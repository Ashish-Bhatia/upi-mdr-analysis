- Issues notice **returnable 2 November 2026**.
- Records that counsel for respondents 1, 3 and 4 accepted notice.

**NOT in the operative written ROP:** a recorded express refusal of interim relief, a direction to suspend the MDR, a ruling on legality or tax status, a ruling of unconstitutionality, or an ultimate disposition. Therefore do not write “the written order refused to stay” as a verbatim or direct legal conclusion.
