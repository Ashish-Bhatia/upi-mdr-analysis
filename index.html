01 / THE INVESTIGATION

What changed.
What hasn't.

The central dispute is not whether payment infrastructure costs money. It does. The dispute is how those costs should be measured, allocated, disclosed and regulated.

THE CENTRAL FINDING

Free at the point of payment is not the same as free to operate.

From January 2020, the Government directed zero merchant discount rate for qualifying UPI and RuPay debit-card transactions. This removed a merchant-facing revenue channel. Public incentives then supported parts of the ecosystem, but the costs of fraud controls, reliability, acceptance, settlement and product development did not disappear.

In August and September 2026, the Government set out an amended legal and policy framework for limited merchant charges. The standard published rate was 0.4% on qualifying P2M transactions above ₹2,000, with exemptions and special tariffs. A planned 15 October launch was subsequently deferred.

That is the documentary sequence. Whether 0.4% is proportionate, whether banks receive an appropriate share, and whether retail prices rise indirectly are separate questions requiring evidence.

THE POLICY CASE

Invest for resilience

A sustainable income source could help providers pay for cyber defence, fraud prevention and distribution.

THE COUNTERCASE

Guard against overcharging

An ad valorem tariff might exceed marginal processing cost, transfer cost to shoppers and reward incumbents.

THE TEST

Publish the underlying numbers

Audited costs, fee-share rules, volume buckets, pass-through data and subsidy outcomes would support a stronger verdict.

DOCUMENT DETAIL 01

The five-percent fund has an exception.

The circular copy calls for 5% of each participant's MDR share towards a small-merchant fund excluding Industry Program merchant categories. This qualification was not present in the initial website edition.

DOCUMENT DETAIL 02

Estimated system cost is not audited cost.

The 42-question NPCI FAQ cites an industry estimate of about ₹20,000 crore annually. It does not establish the exact attributable operating cost or demonstrate that 0.40% is the cost-based rate.

DOCUMENT DETAIL 03

Micro-merchant eligibility is monitored over time.

The FAQ says exceeding ₹1 lakh inward UPI receipts monthly for three consecutive months triggers reclassification from P2PM to P2M. One larger individual payment does not automatically remove the exemption.

MERCHANT MARGIN SENSITIVITY

0.4% of sales is not 0.4% of profit.

If a retailer has a 5% net margin before MDR, a 0.4% payment fee represents 8% of that margin on an affected sale. This is a stylised example. Actual margins, pricing and acceptance mix vary.

07 / METHOD & OPEN QUESTIONS

Evidence first.
Uncertainty visible.

Our approach is reproducible. We distinguish documentary proof, an institution's representation, independent analysis and questions that remain unanswered.

Evidence standard

  1. Identify the exact claimant and date.
  2. Locate the earliest obtainable original record.
  3. Preserve figures with units, base and reporting period.
  4. Test contrary explanations and economic incidence.
  5. Mark findings supported, qualified, contradicted or unresolved.
  6. Record gaps rather than manufacture certainty.

Priority questions

  • Can we independently retrieve the official-domain full OC-237 and Gazette S.O. 5067(E)? Gazette ID and third-party circular copy are identified.
  • What is the actual chargeable P2M value by size and merchant category?
  • What audited cost model justifies 40 basis points and the proposed fee shares?
  • Can the reported 96% exempt-by-count figure be independently reproduced?
  • What changed in the Supreme Court challenge, if anything, beyond reported filing?
  • What is the exact official revised start date after the October postponement?
